BUG FOUND AND FIXED
The first live responses contained extra quotation marks around otherwise exact evidence. The old validator rejected them. Evidence is now checked unchanged first; one matching enclosing quote pair can be removed only when the remaining text exactly occurs in the relevant source title or description. Fabricated evidence, altered punctuation and repeated quote wrappers still fail. Three regression tests were added, and the prompt now requests copied evidence without extra quotation marks and questions only about genuinely missing information.
